ABOUT NEXACITY A CIVIC PLATFORM / 01

Make local issues easier to see, share and follow.

NexaCity is a digital civic reporting platform designed to bring residents and local service workflows into clearer view.

Residents can submit infrastructure concerns with useful details and a location. Administrators can review incoming reports, record status changes and coordinate team assignments through one organised workspace.

Explore how it works ↗
01 / RESIDENT-LED

Start with what people notice.

Give residents a clear way to describe a local issue, choose a category and share the location that matters.

02 / ORGANISED REVIEW

Bring reports into focus.

Help administrators review submissions, understand reported issues and keep recorded actions together.

03 / VISIBLE PROGRESS

Make the next step easier to follow.

Keep report status and its activity history accessible, so residents can see the updates recorded for their reports.
